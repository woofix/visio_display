# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Visio-Display to be a welcoming, respectful and productive project for everyone.

By participating, you agree to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code and project goals.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or disruptive behavior.

## Expected Behavior

- Use clear and considerate language.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification when needed.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and do not share private information without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment, insults, intimidation or threats.
- Discriminatory comments or behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately disruptive or hostile participation.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.

Reports should include:

- a short description of what happened;
- links, screenshots or logs when available;
- any context that may help the maintainers review the situation.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may remove content, limit participation or take any other action needed to protect the project community.
